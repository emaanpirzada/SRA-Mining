ERX9276064	PAIRED	609683	SAMEA14384273	ERS11978662	E-MTAB-11302_2	ERP137579	Spatiotemporal dynamics of CRC metastases resolved by single cell RNAseq	Longitudinal single cell RNA-expression analysis of tumor cells along the metastatic cascade. AKTP MTOs were implanted in the caecum and left to metastasize into the liver. Mice with different metastatic burden were collected and GFP+ CD45- cells were sorted in 96-well smart-seq plates. Tumor cells were collected at four stages (Primary tumors, micrometastases, small metastases and big metastases) and each Smart-seq2 plate contained cells from all conditions to avoid batch effects. Primary tumor samples were matched with micro, small or big metastases samples.
